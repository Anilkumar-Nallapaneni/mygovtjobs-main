# EduPath Resource Policy

## Why this exists

EduPath contains recommendations, links to official sources, and original study material. These must not be presented as if EduPath hosts official or copyrighted PDFs.

## Resource types

1. **Official source** — EduPath links to the authority, board, publisher, university, or examination body. Examples include NCERT, NTA, UPSC and official university/college pages.
2. **EduPath original** — Content generated by the EduPath application itself, such as revision notes, study planners, practice worksheets and career guides.
3. **External resource** — A third-party resource that EduPath links to without copying it.
4. **Catalogue-only** — A recommended book/resource for which EduPath does not provide a downloadable copy.

## Rules

- Never label a generated guide as an official exam paper, textbook, or university document.
- Never download and redistribute copyrighted textbooks or official exam papers unless the rightsholder explicitly permits it.
- Keep the authority/publisher URL beside official resources.
- Show a verification date for time-sensitive official sources.
- When an official URL is unavailable, show the item as a recommendation or generate only original EduPath material.
- Exam practice worksheets must clearly state that they are original EduPath material.
